Inspired by the geometric explanations of 3Blue1Brown. Original graphics and code; not an official reproduction. The 2-D features, weights and tiny vocabulary are deliberately designed, not learned; the attention and softmax calculations are real. A full GPT also includes positional information, learned projections, multiple heads, residual paths, normalization and MLPs.
