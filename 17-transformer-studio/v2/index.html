Model & data, honestly

Trained from scratch on 81 original synthetic sentences, with 15 held-out sentences (5 distinct prefixes). Vocabulary: 21 tokens. Two pre-norm decoder blocks, 2 heads per block, exact 2-D Q/K/V per head, 8-D residual stream, ReLU MLP, learned position embeddings.

No GPT weights. This model learns a small river/financial-bank pattern; it is not a general language model. High confidence here does not establish broad understanding.
