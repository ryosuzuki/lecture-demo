What is this model?
A small trainable, one-head Transformer with causal attention, residual updates and an MLP. The displayed activations, parameter edits, predictions and gradients all belong to the same computation. It is a teaching model—not a pretrained large language model. Repeated updates here optimize the selected example, not general language ability.
