What was checked?

Passing functionality checks is different from proving complete source-video reproduction.No complete-reproduction pass is claimed.

CheckResult
Rendering all 52 scenesBrowser traversal verifies completed rendering, not just scene labels. Checks detect runtime exceptions and targeted off-canvas text.
Numerical and mathematical checks36 checks: stable normalized softmax, T=0, future masking, value/attention independence, score intervention, residuals, output-matrix equivalence, parameter counts, and continuous source-time coverage.
Real browser interactions20 checks: mouse sliders, 3D orbit, word selection, masks and incorrect ordering, play/pause, navigation, classroom mode, and mobile horizontal overflow.
Public pages and recording deliveryLaunch the public lab and seek each of five recordings to three positions, checking actual playback time and byte-range responses.
Recording filesFive H.264 MP4 files, fully decoded, with opening/middle/ending frames inspected. English captions; no audio.
YouTube embedsPlayer loading was verified, but automated playback remained at zero seconds, so playback was not confirmed. Direct links to the source on YouTube are provided.

Previously fixed issues

  • A missing embedding word stopped the rendering loop.
  • Tests could miss stalled rendering when only the scene label changed.
  • Masking was difficult to see; the view now uses the source's separate 6×6 example.
  • Overlapping 3D word labels and camera positions when switching examples.
  • Overlapping query/key descriptions and matrix labels.

Remaining differences

  • Coverage of 52 intervals does not mean every source shot was ported one-to-one.
  • Photos, characters, narration, detailed camera paths, and morph timings differ.
  • Actual GPT-2/3 generation and internal states from the source are not reproduced.
  • Unpublished decimal precision is unavailable; some illustrative values were reconstructed consistently from displayed numbers.
  • Relative to the original request for five distinct versions, this edition is one 52-scene lab plus five walkthroughs, not five complete replicas.
