What this 3D view represents and where its numbers come from

Spatial layout separates tokens, processing stages, and heads. It does not mean 12,288-dimensional embeddings are three-dimensional. Value arrows are three-component teaching examples. The semantic-directions view uses the source's real GloVe 50D data; the new lookup view uses real GPT-2 768D weights and displays its first three components.

The source's eight words and Q/K table orientation are preserved. The single-head view uses explicitly chosen 3D Q/K/V matrices, with scores and updates computed from them. The multi-head gallery retains illustrative source-style attention patterns. Additional head patterns are deterministic teaching data, not GPT-3 measurements. This is not a complete copy of every source effect.
