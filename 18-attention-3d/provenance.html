What was and was not reviewed

Both full transcripts and 320 images sampled at ten-second intervals across the complete duration were reviewed. Major numerical tables were checked against extra full-resolution frames and public code. This does not mean both animations were watched continuously with every motion verified.

The 52 mapped intervals cover the full duration, but not every shot within each interval was separately ported. Photos, characters, actual GPT-2/3 generation, announcements, and supporter credits are not fully reproduced. Each scene provides an embed of the source on the author's YouTube channel.

Parameter countsArithmetic using published GPT-3 175B dimensions and the source's large-matrix counting convention. Exact totals for all biases and normalization parameters are not separately reconstructed.

Equation notation

Column-vector convention: A = softmax_columns(KᵀQ / √dₖ + mask), ΔE = V A. The source notes notational conveniences; this executable lab consistently uses shape-compatible matrices.
