# Teaching guide

## Recommended picks

1. **Observe → Act** (5 minutes): strongest opening. Set World changes to 1, Feedback frequency to 0, ask for a prediction, run, then increase feedback and rerun.
2. **Context Packing** (6 minutes): strongest discussion. Increase history until evidence is displaced; ask what to compress or remove.
3. **Coordination Cost** (6 minutes): strongest closing. Compare one and three agents under low, then high, handoff cost.

## Other experiments

- **Plan, Fail, Replan:** compare fixed and adaptive plans under two disruptions. Replanning reacts to new observations but adds cost.
- **Memory Mixer:** ask students to choose three memories before moving relevance/recency weights. Retrieval is selection, not guaranteed truth.
- **Trust, but Verify:** increase checks, then lower check accuracy. A verifier has limits and consumes budget.

## Truth boundary

All six are deterministic conceptual simulations, not benchmark results and not live LLM inference. Displayed events are externally designed system states. They should never be described as access to a model's hidden chain of thought.
